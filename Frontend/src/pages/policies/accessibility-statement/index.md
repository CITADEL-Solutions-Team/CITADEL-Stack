# Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 2, 2026

At Citadel Solutions, we believe that cybersecurity is a right for everyone, not a privilege for a few. This commitment extends to our digital presence. We are dedicated to ensuring our website is accessible to all users, regardless of their abilities, assistive technology, or circumstances.

## 1. Our Commitment

- Ensuring all users can navigate our site using keyboard-only controls.
- Providing text alternatives for non-text content (images, icons, charts).
- Ensuring our site is compatible with screen readers and other assistive technologies.
- Maintaining sufficient color contrast and scalable text for users with visual impairments.
- Designing clear, predictable navigation and error identification for cognitive accessibility.

## 2. Conformance Status

The Citadel Solutions website conforms to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. This assessment is based on a combination of manual testing (keyboard navigation, screen reader use, zoom, and small-screen layouts) and automated testing.

- Navigation: Fully keyboard navigable, with a skip link to bypass repeated content and labeled navigation regions for screen readers.
- Content: Images and icons have appropriate text alternatives, and text meets minimum color contrast requirements.
- Forms: All fields have visible labels, required fields are identified, and submission results are announced to screen readers.
- Media: Our website does not currently contain video or audio content.

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you encounter any barriers or find content that is difficult to access, please let us know so we can address it immediately.

## 4. Technical Specifications

Our website is designed to be compatible with the following assistive technologies and browsers:

- Screen Readers: JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack
- Browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest two versions)
- Zoom Levels: Fully functional up to 200% without loss of content or functionality

## 5. Third-Party Content

This website may link to third-party services or contain embedded content (e.g., video players, social media feeds) that we do not control. While we strive to partner with accessible vendors, we cannot guarantee the accessibility of external sites. If you encounter an accessibility issue with a third-party link, please contact us, and we will do our best to assist.

## 6. Alternative Formats

If you are unable to access specific content on our website, we are happy to provide alternative formats upon request. Please contact our support team with your specific needs.

## 7. Monitoring and Improvement

We regularly monitor our website for accessibility issues using automated tools and manual testing. We are committed to maintaining conformance as our website changes, and we will update this statement whenever our conformance status changes.

This statement was created to align with WCAG 2.1 AA standards. For specific technical details regarding our implementation, please contact us at <legal@citadelsolutions.tech>.

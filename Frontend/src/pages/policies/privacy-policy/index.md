# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Citadel Solutions LLC ("we," "us," or "our") provides website development, cybersecurity services, and PC repair services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit citadelsolutions.tech (the "Site") or use our services.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect contact information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and any other details you submit through contact forms, service inquiries, or when requesting a quote.

When you visit the Site, our hosting and security provider also automatically processes technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver and protect the Site.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

- Respond to inquiries and provide quotes.
- Deliver website development, cybersecurity, and PC repair services.
- Communicate with you about your service requests.
- Maintain records for invoicing and business purposes.

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not share your information with advertising networks or data brokers.

We use a limited number of service providers that process information on our behalf, solely to operate the Site and our services:

- Cloudflare: security, content delivery, and privacy-focused website analytics. Cloudflare processes technical information such as IP addresses.
- Brevo: email delivery. When you submit our contact form, Brevo processes the information you provide so that we can receive and respond to your message.

We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect our rights, property, or safety.

## 4. Payment Information

We do not collect or process payment information through this Site. Payment for services is arranged and invoiced separately, outside of the website.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain the contact information you provide for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information you provide from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
